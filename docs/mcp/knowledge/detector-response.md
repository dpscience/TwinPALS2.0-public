# Module 1 · Detector response

The detector setup determines where gamma energy is deposited and how much scintillation or Cherenkov light reaches each photocathode. Inspect the source and samples, detector positions, scintillator shape and material, wrapping, coupling, optical window, cathode and PMT response for A and B. The minimum crystal energy and Cherenkov switch are recorded with the run.

The transport stream stores accepted photoelectrons and ancestry. Cathode arrival is measured after gamma emission. PMT onset includes the calibrated transit delay and time spread. The analog response sums single-photoelectron kernels using the recorded charge, gain, polarity and effective anode load. In the PMT Response tab, `spe_charge_spread_percent` is the standard deviation of the charge from one accepted photoelectron, as a percentage of its mean eG. The numbered references identify measured widths, family transfers and estimates. New runs with a positive value draw independent, positive charges and keep the calibrated Erlang shape, dynode count and single-PE transit-time spread. Missing or zero spread and older fixed-charge stream snapshots give every electron charge eG. The frozen response seed reproduces the same anode pulse throughout transport, acquisition and analysis. The two times in a photoelectron record describe different stages of the same detection.

Module 1 Setup stores an optional anode pulse shape factor separately for detectors A and B. Set `pmt_shape_enabled` and `pmt_shape_factor` on the detector. The default is off with factor 1. The effective gamma order is the number of dynodes multiplied by the factor and must lie between 2 and 32. The scale is recalibrated to the reference 10–90% rise time and the kernel retains unit area. The selected shape can change the pulse width, peak height and CFD time. Gain sets charge and the catalogue TTS still governs each electron’s arrival. A fractional order is recorded in the immutable response for waveform replay.

Each detector has a `pmt_cathode_reflections` setup switch (default true, including older setup profiles). The PMT catalogue entry must enable `cathode_reflections` and supply its referenced film data. When both switches are enabled, Geant4 follows photon reflections from the active front face using wavelength, incidence angle, polarization, film thickness and complex index. Absorbed photons produce electrons with an efficiency calibrated at normal incidence to the stored QE reference plane. Reflected photons can return through the detector. Geant4 transports linear polarization. The film response samples the reflected linear components and does not retain circular polarization history. Light transmitted into the tube interior is lost. Outside the common measured index range, the boundary uses QE only. With reflections enabled for that detector, the second spectrum view shows normal-incidence reflection, absorption and transmission at the active film, alongside the selected scintillator’s emission spectrum scaled to its own peak. The film percentages retain their absolute scale.

Na-22 supplies a prompt gamma role and an annihilation-pair role. Co-60 has two cascade roles whose matched records must stay paired. Consult the isotope and role fields before combining files. A zero-response emission and an event producing several accepted photoelectrons have different effects on acquisition efficiency.

Example. To test a faster PMT, keep source, optics and geometry fixed, change the PMT operating point or model supported by the profile, and run transport again. Inspect the accepted photoelectron counts, response timing and saved PMT snapshot before comparing lifetime resolution. A change made to today's catalogue cannot retime an existing transport file.


Module 1 Analysis shows **Optical reflections** in both Crystal and Anode. **Crystal / window** counts reflected steps starting inside the crystal or its entrance window. **Photocathode** counts reflected steps at the active film, from glass or a vacuum gap. One event contributes its count, including repeated reflections and photons that never produce a photoelectron. Crystal includes eligible events with zero pulse. Anode requires a pulse in the same detector. The API metric names are `reflections` and `cathode_reflections`.
